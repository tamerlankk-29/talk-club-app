# Admin guide — everyday tasks

For club staff. Sign in at `/login` with your email and password; you'll land in the admin
panel (`/admin`). Press **Ctrl + K** (⌘ + K) anywhere to search clients, leads, teachers,
payments and memberships.

## The dashboard

Shows today's sessions and occupancy, attendance, active clients, revenue (if you have
finance access) and **alerts**: pending payments, sessions with unmarked attendance,
memberships expiring soon, overdue tasks, waitlists, new trials and data requests. Click an
alert to jump to the list.

## Selling a membership (Kaspi invoice)

1. Open the client (search) → **Payment request**.
2. Choose the plan and "Kaspi invoice" → **Create request**. The client gets a notification.
3. Send the Kaspi invoice from the Kaspi business app as usual.
4. When it's paid: Payments → filter **Pending** → open the payment → **Confirm payment** and
   enter the Kaspi reference. The membership activates immediately and the client is notified.

Paid in cash or by card at the desk? In the payment request tick **Payment already received**
— it's recorded and activated in one step.

Clients can also start a purchase themselves in the app; it appears as a pending payment for
you to confirm.

## Booking a client into a session

Schedule → open the session → **Add participant** → find the client. The visit is taken from
their membership automatically. Managers with override permission can book over capacity or
outside the level range (the booking is marked).

## Cancellations and moves

On the session page, open the participant's menu:

- **Cancel booking** — before the free-cancellation deadline (20:00 the day before) the visit
  returns automatically; after it the visit is used. With override permission you can tick
  "Return the visit even if it's a late cancellation" (e.g. illness). A reason is required.
- **Move to another session** — keeps the visit and notifies the client.

## Cancelling a session

Session page → **Cancel session** → give a reason. Every booked client gets their visit back
and a notification; the waitlist is cleared.

## Attendance

- Teachers mark attendance in their panel; you can mark or correct any session in
  **Attendance** (one card per session, tap Present / Late / No-show, or "All present").
- Unmarked bookings become no-shows automatically 3 hours after the session.
- To return a visit for a no-show (exceptional cases), open the client → Memberships → the
  membership → **Adjust visits** (+1) with a reason.

## Weekly schedule

Schedule → **Weekly templates**: one template per recurring slot (days, time, room, teacher,
level, group type). Sessions are generated 4 weeks ahead every night. Editing a template
updates future sessions you haven't edited by hand; sessions with bookings are never deleted.
One-off sessions: Schedule → **New session**, or click an empty slot in the calendar. The day
view shows rooms side by side — conflicts are impossible (the system refuses double-booked
rooms or teachers).

## Memberships

Client → **Memberships** → open one to see the full **visit ledger** (every booking,
cancellation and correction). Actions (reason required, all audited): **Extend**, **Change
expiry**, **Adjust visits**, **Cancel**. **Grant membership** gives one without payment
(gift, compensation, migration).

## Trials

**Trials** lists requests from the website/app. **Schedule** puts the person into a session
(they're notified). After the session record **Attended** / **No-show**, then the **outcome**.
Automations create follow-up tasks.

## CRM

- **CRM pipeline:** drag cards between stages; most stages also move automatically from real
  events. Click a card for details, communications, tasks and "Create client profile".
- **Tasks:** your follow-ups (some created automatically, e.g. renewal calls, inactive clients).
- On a client page: **Activity** (timeline + notes), communications log (WhatsApp, calls,
  Instagram…), tags, tasks, feedback, family and privacy.

## Marketing

- **Segments:** saved groups of clients built from rules (membership status, last visit, level,
  tags, lead source…) with a live count.
- **Broadcasts:** one-off messages to a segment via in-app, push, WhatsApp, Telegram, SMS or
  email — send now or schedule. Marketing broadcasts only reach clients who opted in;
  service messages (closures, schedule changes) reach everyone in the audience.
- **Announcements:** cards on the client home screen, optionally targeted and time-limited.
- **Automations:** "when X happens (or every day for Y), if the client matches, do Z" — e.g.
  remind 7/3/1 days before expiry, follow up after a trial, win back inactive clients. Each rule
  can be switched off; the activity log shows every run.
- **Notifications:** edit the wording and channels of every automatic message; send yourself a
  test.

## Reports and exports

**Reports** — overview KPIs, revenue (by day/week/month, product, method), clients, memberships
(sales, renewals), sessions (occupancy, no-shows, best days and times), teachers and the
conversion funnel. **Import & export** — download clients, bookings, attendance, memberships,
leads, payments or revenue as Excel/CSV; import clients or Altegio history (always run the
preview first).

## Settings

Club profile, booking and cancellation rules, sessions and attendance, memberships, trials and
waitlist, notifications and staff alerts, CRM thresholds and referrals, branding colours, app
content (About, How it works, Rules, FAQ), legal documents (versioned), location, opening
hours and rooms, levels/group types/lead sources, payment methods. Changes apply immediately
and are audited.

## Staff and roles

**Staff & roles** (owner): add staff (a temporary password is shown once), assign roles, block
access, reset passwords; edit what each role may do. Teachers get their login from **Teachers**
→ teacher → **Create login**.

## Privacy requests

**Import & export → Data requests** lists deletion/export requests. To complete a deletion,
open the client → Family & privacy → **Anonymize (GDPR)**. To decline, give a reason (logged).
